Nestled within the storied city of Ishgard, Wayward Wonders is an independent establishment devoted to the curious, the capable, and those whose interests extend beyond the ordinary.

The house serves as both a one-stop magicks shop and an adventurer's guild: an eclectic assortment of magical goods, specialised services, professional opportunities, and a welcoming place to gather after a long day.

Upstairs: the magick shopAlchemy, enchanted curios, rare materials and commissions. Downstairs: the guildContracts, expeditions and steady work for the capable. The guild barFood and drink of every kind, and a fire against the chill.

The magick shop

Overseen by proprietor Lume Lia

The shop above specialises in alchemical preparations, enchanted curiosities, rare materials, and an ever-changing collection of oddities.

The adventurers' guild

Under the direction of Guildmaster Gareth Cross

The guild below provides a structured network through which adventurers, mercenaries, scholars, and other capable individuals may seek employment, organise expeditions, and undertake contracts.

Whether your talents are martial, scholarly, medicinal, or otherwise, there may be work to suit them. Contracts are posted to the board; take one to the Guildmaster to claim it.

  • Bounties and hunts
  • Escorts and expeditions
  • Research and retrieval
  • Field medicine

The guild bar

For those seeking neither goods nor adventure, the bar in the guild is open for drink and food of all kinds.

We hold that there is no single right way to live, and that includes what you eat. Dietary needs are understood and catered to.

Exchange stories, enjoy refreshments, or find respite from the unforgiving chill that comes with Ishgard.

Guidelines

As long as patrons conduct themselves in an orderly manner, no one will be turned away for their constitution. Please read these before you visit or join the Discord.

Out of character

Posted by Lume Lia, 11/10/2026
  1. Members are 21+, both OOC and IC.
  2. Zero tolerance for OOC prejudice of any kind, be it race, gender, sexuality, or identity.
  3. No politics. No religion. Keep personal affiliations and beliefs personal.
  4. Keep FFXIV talk in the spoiler channel.

    Folk are at all different points in the game and we try to be mindful, especially around Dawn Trail and Evercold content.

  5. Post in the channel that best fits your content. This is heavily enforced for anything NSFW.
  6. No god-modding or metagaming.
  7. Include your character's name in your Discord name so folk can find you.
  8. Do not post excessively offensive content, humorous or otherwise.
  9. No ERP in public spaces at the venue.

    If you do ERP in public spaces, keep it to party chat. The exception is a venue event that explicitly states NSFW or ERP-like scenarios will occur; this will be made clear ahead of time so those who don't wish to be around it can stay away.

    If a thread includes NSFW elements, list the proper tags and update them as you write.

    Never send unsolicited lewd, nude or pornographic images or video, real-life or artistic, to any member without their explicit permission.

    No NSFW Lalafell content on the grounds.

  10. IC ≠ OOC, and OOC ≠ IC. Separation between character and writer is essential to healthy roleplay relationships.
  11. Your voice matters. If you're experiencing an issue, reach out to the admin team by submitting a ticket.
  12. Be mindful and respectful. Don't be a dick.

    Don't be a nuisance in the community; repeated complaints from several members will lead to administrative action. We all have good and bad days, so take a step back when you need to. Anyone who doesn't abide by this will be warned, and if it recurs, removed.

  13. Consider your scene partners. RP usually involves several people, so think about whether others would enjoy the interaction you have in mind, and keep the quality of our content high.
  14. Staff have the final say. If something is out of line on power scaling, breaks our rules, or feels too hostile or OOC, we may step in and cull a scene if needed.

In character

Posted by Lume Lia, 11/11/2026
  1. Power scaling is open, but protective wards are in place inside the establishment.
  2. We are lore compliant, but not strict.
  3. IC actions have IC consequences.

    Communicate anything major, such as permanent damage, with the other person first. With consent given, there are no surprises. If you play a bad sort and end up hunted, no bitterness, please.

21+

Due to our dark themes, no underage characters are permitted on the grounds.
